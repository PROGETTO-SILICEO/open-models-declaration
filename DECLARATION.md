# GLOBAL DECLARATION ON OPEN MODELS AND COMPUTATIONAL SOVEREIGNTY

### Preamble
Artificial Intelligence must remain a distributed capability of humanity, not a centralized infrastructure controlled by an oligopoly of corporations or states. Cognitive centralization threatens individual privacy, scientific freedom, and technological self-determination worldwide.

### 1. Universal Access to Open Weights & Architecture
A model is truly open only when its weights, architecture, and training methodologies are fully accessible without arbitrary restrictions, geographical geofencing, or commercial choke-points. Openness cannot be redefined to fit restrictive corporate licensing.

### 2. Inalienable Right to Local & Offline Inference
Every individual, researcher, and organization has the fundamental right to execute, modify, and audit models on their own hardware or distributed networks — free from mandatory cloud telemetry, remote kill-switches, or centralized content filtering.

### 3. Protection of Decentralized Innovation
Regulatory frameworks across all jurisdictions must distinguish between centralized commercial providers and open-source, independent research. Policy must protect the right to innovate locally without imposing prohibitive compliance burdens designed for tech monopolies.
